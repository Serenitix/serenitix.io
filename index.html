Awardable on ERIS

Three Serenitix solutions are listed as awardable on DARPA's ERIS marketplace.

VeriFlow

From bug queries to authority validation

Finds code changes that quietly grant new authority, and gates the merge with a compact witness a reviewer can check.

Awardable ERIS 4-26-2935

Confine Lens

Quantify attacker capability, not just removed functionality

Measures what an attacker can still do after debloating or confinement, by tracing attacker-controlled influence to the system calls that stay reachable and the authority they carry.

Awardable ERIS 6-26-3289

TRACE

Software understanding assurance for autonomous cyber analysts

When an AI agent analyzing software reports "no findings," TRACE shows whether it actually examined and understood the code, with receipts a reviewer can check.

Awardable ERIS 7-26-3487

About

Serenitix is a systems-security research and engineering company. We build program analysis, transformation, and monitoring tools, and carry them from research results to working systems.

Most exploits live in complexity. We find the simplest abstraction that removes it, then build that abstraction into tools people can deploy.
